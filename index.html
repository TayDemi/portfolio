Email classification workflow

n8n, Gmail, Slack, Airtable, JavaScript

Problem.A customer emails a company and hears nothing for hours while the message sits in a general inbox, waiting to be forwarded. Slow replies cost sales and frustrate customers.

What I built. An n8n workflow that watches a Gmail inbox, decides which department each email belongs to, and routes it there. A Switch step sends it down one of nine branches. Each branch posts the message to that department’s Slack channel, replies to the sender to confirm, and logs a ticket in Airtable.

Result. In testing, a membership inquiry reached the Sales Slack channel, the sender received an acknowledgement, and a ticket was logged in Airtable.

Gym membership automation

n8n, Airtable, Gmail, webhooks, imgbb API, Postman

Problem. A gym loses members the moment a subscription quietly expires and nobody notices. This system catches every sign-up, warns members a week before expiry, and records renewals automatically

What I built. Three connected n8n workflows. Registration: a form submission arrives by webhook, an optional photo is uploaded to imgbb, the member is created in Airtable, and a welcome email and an owner alert go out. Reminders: every day at 9am it finds subscriptions expiring within a week, emails the member and marks the record. Renewals: a payment-success request calculates the new end date, updates Airtable, and emails both the member and the gym manager.

Result. In testing with Postman, a renewal request updated the subscription in Airtable and sent confirmation emails to the member and the gym manager.

PCOS prediction model

Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Streamlit

Problem. Predict whether a patient has PCOS from clinical and hormonal measurements in a public dataset.

What I built. A classification pipeline that cleans clinical data, selects features with cross-validation, and trains a Random Forest, with a Streamlit app that runs the saved model. The model uses 20 inputs, including cycle details, hormone levels such as LH, FSH/LH and AMH, and follicle counts.

Result. On 109 held-out records never used for training or tuning, the model reached 90% accuracy, 83% recall and a ROC AUC of 0.95. It caught 30 of 36 PCOS cases and flagged 5 people without PCOS.

Limits. Follicle counts and cycle regularity are part of how PCOS is defined, so strong scores on this dataset partly reflect that. This is a learning project, not a diagnostic tool.
